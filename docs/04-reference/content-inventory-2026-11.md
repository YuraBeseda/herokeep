### Species / races
2024 PHB: 10 species [M, one search source says 10]: Aasimar, Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Goliath, Halfling, Human, Orc, Tiefling. SRD 5.2.1 / pack: 9 (all but Aasimar). Elf (Drow, High, Wood lineages), Gnome (Forest, Rock lineages), Tiefling (Abyssal, Chthonic, Infernal legacies), Dragonborn (10 ancestries), Goliath (giant ancestries), Dwarf has none (Duergar etc. no longer subraces).
BG3 mapping: Human P; Elf (High/Wood) P via lineage; Drow P via Elf lineage (Drow lineage is in PHB 2024, as Elf); Half-Elf, Half-Orc: removed in 2024 (use Human/Elf/Orc + custom origin in 2024 or the 2014 "Half-Elf"/"Half-Orc" as legacy); Halfling Lightfoot/Strongheart: 2024 Halfling has no subrace (Lightfoot/Stout gone); Dwarf Gold/Shield/Duergar: 2024 has one Dwarf; Gnome Forest/Rock/Deep: Forest and Rock lineages in 2024 PHB, Deep Gnome not; Tiefling Asmodeus/Mephistopheles/Zariel: 2024 Infernal legacy (one legacy); Githyanki: not in 2024 PHB (Mordenkainen's Monsters of the Multiverse 2022 / Spelljammer-era; no 2024 book yet); Dragonborn colours P.
Builder differences: 2024 species give NO ability scores (these come from background); lineages are choices inside a species; size/speed/darkvision fixed per species; languages come from background/class not species.

### Backgrounds
2024 PHB: 16 backgrounds [M, consistent with dungeonmister table]: Acolyte, Artisan, Charlatan, Criminal, Entertainer, Farmer, Guard, Guide, Hermit, Merchant, Noble, Sage, Sailor, Scribe, Soldier, Wayfarer. SRD 5.2.1 and pack: 4 (Acolyte, Criminal, Sage, Soldier). Note: official SRD page says "3 added" (Criminal, Sage, Soldier) on top of 5.1's Acolyte, total 4.
BG3 mapping: Acolyte P, Charlatan, Criminal P, Entertainer, Guild Artisan -> Artisan, Noble, Sage P, Soldier P, Sailor/Hermit(removed in BG3, in 2024), Folk Hero -> Farmer (closest; 2024 dropped Folk Hero), Outlander -> Wayfarer/Guide (closest), Urchin -> Wayfarer(closest), Haunted One -> none (Curse of Strahd 2014 only).
Builder differences: 2024 background grants +2/+1 or +1/+1/+1 ability scores from a three-ability list, one origin feat, two skills, one tool; 2014 backgrounds gave two skills, tools/languages and a flavour feature, no ability scores or feat.
