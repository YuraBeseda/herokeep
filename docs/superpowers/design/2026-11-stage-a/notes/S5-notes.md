# S5 — "Later ideas" boards (concept sketches, not in scope)

Ten light concept sketches in the approved "Tavern Table" language, so the owner can see how each later idea would look and a later planner can judge its cost. Nothing here is scheduled; every board is a fixed artboard so it reads as a sketch, not a screen. Group id S5. All boards are English (`lang="en"`), as the group brief asked, including the phone moment.

Authoring: `*.src.html` are the sources; `node build.cjs` expands the macros (`<i-icon>`, `<x-seal>`, `<x-tex>` as in `out/A2/build.cjs`; `<x-defs>`, `<x-shield>`, `<x-mono>`, `<x-coin>`, `<x-d20>` added here), inlines `ds/check/tokens.css` + `ds/project/components/bundle.css` + `s5.css` into each `<helmet><style>`, stamps `$preview` with the board's fixed frame, and asserts there is no template syntax, `data:` URI, emoji or `!important` in the output. `node shoot.cjs` renders every `.dc.html` at its own frame size, screenshots it and probes for clipped text and anything spilling past the frame (the only reports left are screen-reader-only labels, which are clipped by design). Every board was rendered and looked at at least three times; the last run reports no clipping and no spill.

## What each idea needs (one line each)

| Idea | Needs |
|---|---|
| Companion mini-sheet | **engine + protocol + packs**: a companion entity on the hero (HP, AC, speed, abilities, attacks, traits, carried items) with summon / dismiss / damage / heal events on the hero's stream, carried by `.hero` export and shown on the DM's party card; creature stat blocks come from packs, so the pack format needs a `creature` entity type (the SRD pack has none; Find Steed's Otherworldly Steed and the Warhorse are not entities today) |
| Wild Shape switcher | **engine + packs** (protocol for the shift / revert events): a form that replaces STR / DEX / CON, AC, speed, attacks and senses while keeping HP, Hit Dice, INT / WIS / CHA, proficiencies and features, grants temp HP = Druid level, locks spellcasting, keeps concentration and conditions; uses the pack's existing `wild-shape` resource; beast stat blocks again need the `creature` entity type |
| DM encounter board | **UI only** (the owner's call: last priority): DM-local initiative order, enemy rows (name, HP, AC, conditions) and turn pointer kept on the DM's device; it hooks into the encounter / round events already decided for Stage B (§4.3); sharing the order with players would add one campaign event (protocol) |
| Loot and coin split | **protocol (campaign events) + a little engine**: a per-campaign loot list, give-item and give-coins events that land on each hero's stream (the DM "Item" quick effect and coin events exist), a split helper with remainder handling, and a player-side claim request |
| Session journal and quests | **protocol (campaign events)**: journal entries with author, timestamp, visibility (party / only me) and tags; NPC and quest entities with status; search is client-side; no engine work |
| DM shop | **protocol + engine**: shop open / close and item-price events on the campaign; a purchase is two hero events (coins paid with change-making across pp / gp / sp / cp, item added) plus a campaign ledger line; pack list prices come from item `cost` data already in the pack |
| Death-saves moment | **UI only**: death saves, stabilise and healing already exist in the engine; this is a full-screen presentation of the existing state on the phone |
| Printable sheet | **UI only**: a print stylesheet (no textures, black ink), the optional spells page and the blank-boxes variant; Save PDF is the browser's print-to-PDF |
| 3D dice | **UI only**: a rendering layer over the existing roll service (same RNG, same results); honours reduced motion; a setting with three modes |
| Theme packs | **UI only for Tavern / Scroll / Plain** (they are the existing token sets); other-game packs need the **pack format** to carry a `theme` kind (tokens.json, fonts, ornament switches) — protocol, no engine |

## Content choices and invented strings

- Ilyana Vorn and every number on her boards follow `CONTENT.md`; party members (Bramble Underfoot, Sister Maren, Thorne Ashvale, Oskar Bellow, Guard captain) and the campaign reuse S3's roster. Item names, prices, rarities and descriptions are the SRD pack's (`Potion of Healing` 50 gp, `Healer's Kit` 5 gp, `Rations` 5 sp, `Torch` 1 cp, `Lantern, Hooded` 5 gp, `Rope` 1 gp, `Javelin` 5 sp, `Chain Mail` 75 gp, `Warhorse` 400 gp, `Cloak of Protection`, `Pearl of Power`, `Spyglass` 1,000 gp); the Find Steed and Wild Shape texts are the pack's.
- Invented for the sketches (not in CONTENT.md or the pack): the druid **Tamsin Oakhollow** (Human · Druid 8 · Circle of the Land — real species and subclass; level 8 so that a CR 1 Brown Bear is a legal form per the pack's Beast Shapes table, 3 Wild Shape uses, 8 temporary HP); NPC names **Foreman Hald, Captain Veska, Old Mirel**; quest names; the shop name "Trading post"; the custom enemy "Goblin Boss"; session dates and times. Creature numbers come from the SRD 5.1 stat blocks (Giant Spider AC 14 / 26 HP, Goblin AC 15 / 7 HP, Brown Bear AC 11, speed 40 ft, climb 30 ft, STR 19 DEX 10 CON 16, Bite +5 1d8+4, Claws +5 2d6+4, Keen Smell, Multiattack) and from the 2024 Otherworldly Steed at spell level 2 (AC 12, 25 HP, speed 60 ft, Otherworldly Slam at your spell attack bonus for 1d8 + 4 radiant, Life Bond, STR 18 DEX 12 CON 14 INT 6 WIS 12 CHA 8) — none of these are pack entities yet, which is exactly the `creature` gap noted above.
- Wild Shape follows the pack's 2024 text: HP and Hit Dice are kept, the form grants temporary HP equal to the Druid level, and dropping to 0 HP ends the form through the Incapacitated condition. The brief's wording ("the beast's HP strap separate … dropping to 0 HP") was drawn as the separate temporary-HP strap next to the kept HP strap, with the 0-HP consequence in the Revert tooltip. If the owner wants the 2014 behaviour (separate beast HP, carry-over damage) the strap simply shows the beast's HP instead; the layout does not change.
- The steed has one attack (the stat block has one), so the Companion board shows one attack row; the two-attack pattern (Bite + Claws) is on the Wild Shape board.

## Hint rule, states, accessibility

- No legends anywhere; every icon-only control carries `aria-label` + `title`, every meaning glyph a hint. One tooltip is drawn open per board where it teaches something: the "companion" chip (Companion), Revert (Wild Shape), Next turn (Encounter), Split evenly (Loot), the private entry's "only me" chip (Journal), Open to party (Shop), the third failure seal (Death saves), Print 2 pages (Print), the 3D-dice toggle (Dice), Add pack (Themes).
- Disabled with a reason on hover: Summon while the steed is out (Companion), the locked Spells tab (Wild Shape), Give with no hero picked and the pregen's share chip (Loot), Buy on the 400 gp Warhorse (Shop). Error: the "shop closed" toast (Shop). Empty: "From a pack · 0 creatures" in the Add enemy popover (Encounter). Loading: the "Loading 3 more from session 11" button (Journal). Focus ring shown on a monogram chip (Loot), "Buy for 50 gp" (Shop) and Done (Dice).
- Real elements throughout (`button`, `a`, `input`, `select`, `textarea` with labels); phone targets 44–56 px; desktop targets ≥ 28 px icon buttons inside rows, ≥ 36 px elsewhere; text on parchment and leather uses the design-system token pairs (≥ 4.5:1); state colours always pair with an icon or shape (check / cross on the death seals, tombstone on "down", padlock on private).
- Icons: game-icons only; the kit's `icons.json` plus these taken from the cloned repo the same way A2 did: `shop`, `price-tag`, `shopping-cart`, `telescope`, `ringed-planet`, `fedora`, `hand-ok`, `goblin-head`, `next-button`, `papers`, `claws`, `hoof`, `pocket-watch`, `satellite`, `rocket`, `moon-orbit`, `newspaper`, `trample`, `dice-target`, `fast-forward-button`, `saddle`, `hazard-sign`, `clockwise-rotation`, `checklist`, `dice-eight-faces-eight`, `third-eye`, `thrown-spear` (only the ones a board uses are inlined; `icons-used.json` lists them with authors). Authors used: badges, carl-olsen, darkzaitzev, delapouite, lorc, lucasms, sbed, skoll, willdabeast.

## Compromises

- Fixed 720 × 900 artboards (390 × 844 for the phone moment) instead of the 1440 fluid page: the brief asks for sketches, so the shell (brass rail, action strap) is omitted and each board carries one title plate naming the idea; the hero header on the Companion, Wild Shape and Themes boards is a condensed folio strip, not the full sheet.
- The print page is a 540 × 764 preview (A4 ratio) with 9–10 px type: it is a picture of paper, not UI text, so it sits below the 14 px desktop floor on purpose; the real print uses the paper's own scale.
- The d20 is a shaded icosahedron face view drawn in SVG with two blurred ghosts for the tumble; a real 3D renderer would animate it.
- The Observatory and Casefile rows are token overrides on top of the design-system classes (`[data-theme="observatory"]`, `[data-theme="casefile"]` in `s5.css`); a few row-local inline tweaks (square corners, uppercase tabs, the rotated stamp) stand in for ornament tokens that do not exist yet.
- Hints are native `title` / SVG `<title>` plus static open tooltips; the 300 ms hover / 400 ms long-press timing is for the build.
- The encounter board shares the party rows' numbers with S3 but its enemy rows are DM-typed, as the vision rules out monster libraries beyond packs; the pack search shows its empty state for that reason.
