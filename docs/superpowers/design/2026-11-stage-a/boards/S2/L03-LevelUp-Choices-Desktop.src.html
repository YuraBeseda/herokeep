Level 8: feat or Ability Score Improvement

one or the other; feats you already have and feats you don’t qualify for stay listed with the reason

Ability Score Improvement

Increase one ability score by 2, or two scores by 1 each. No score can go above 20.

Strength 16 → 18: modifier +3 → +4. Longsword to hit +6 → +7, damage 1d8+3 → 1d8+4, Athletics +6 → +7.

Or take a feat

Choosing a feat clears the improvement on the left, and the other way round.
