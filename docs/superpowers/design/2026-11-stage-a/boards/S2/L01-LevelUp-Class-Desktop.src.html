Which class?

Your own class is recommended. Multiclassing needs Strength 13 and Charisma 13 for Paladin (met) plus the new class’s scores.
Or start a second class (level 1 in that class; your Paladin stays at 5)
Classes that need a score you lack stay listed; the reason shows on hover.

Hit points for level 6

Roll the Paladin hit die or take its average; Constitution +2 is added either way.
